Multi-Agent Research System

How Nexus Search Works

Six specialized AI agents collaborate in a sequential pipeline to deliver deep, accurate, and structured research reports on any topic imaginable.

🔭

Scout

Maps the research landscape, identifies key subtopics and angles before deep investigation begins.

📚

Researcher

Performs comprehensive multi-domain research with depth, breadth, and multi-perspective coverage.

📊

Analyst

Extracts patterns, generates data visualizations, and produces quantitative insights with charts.

🔍

Critic

Validates findings, cross-checks claims, identifies gaps, and ensures research integrity throughout.

🧠

Synthesist

Combines all agent outputs into a coherent structure, building narrative and identifying key themes.

✍️

Writer

Crafts the final report with clarity, structure, executive summary, and actionable takeaways.
